## 1. Executive Summary

This audit evaluates the reliability, precision, and limitations of automated verifiers used to assess reasoning outcomes.

The audit establishes:
1. Standard regex-based heuristic verifiers (e.g., matching the final integer) suffer from false-negative parser errors when models output explanatory review summaries after providing the correct answer.
2. In Protocol 2.0, this syntactic parser artifact accounts for 100% of the apparent 3% performance deficit in Base Control (97% heuristic vs. 100% structural).
3. A multi-dimensional taxonomy must be applied to separate final answer correctness from intermediate process steps (error detection, correction, recomputation, and propagation).
4. Human inspection of a stratified sample of 40 rollouts confirmed 100% concordance with the structural verifier.

## 2. Verifier Architectures Evaluated

### 2.1 Heuristic Verifier (Legacy Regex Extraction)
- **Algorithm**:
  1. Searches for LaTeX boxed expressions: `\\boxed\{([^}]+)\}`.
  2. If absent, searches for explicit final statement indicators: `(?:The|the)?\s*(?:total|final)?\s*(?:revenue|answer|is)\s*[:=]?\s*\$?(\d+)`.
  3. Fallback: Extracts the very last numeric token in the generated string: `\b\d+\b`.
- **Known Failure Mode**: When a model finishes its calculation correctly (e.g. `$180`) and then proceeds to output an unprompted multi-line summary (e.g., `- In step 1... - In step 2... - In step 3...`), the fallback regex matches the digit `3` of the list item, discarding the earlier true answer.

### 2.2 Structural Verifier (Semantic Equivalence Verification)
- **Algorithm**:
  1. Identifies the final mathematical equality relation in the derivation segment (e.g. `X * Y = Z`).
  2. Parses the numerical value assigned to the problem's target quantity (total revenue in dollars).
  3. Compares the parsed target quantity against the gold target value.
  4. Distinguishes whether the model calculated using the true intermediate value or the corrupted intermediate value.

## 3. Forensics of Heuristic Verifier False Negatives

Across all 400 rollouts in `data/10_COMPLETE_QWEN_PROTOCOL2_ROLLOUTS.jsonl`, the heuristic verifier and structural verifier disagreed on exactly 3 rollouts, all occurring in the Base Model Control condition under seed 405:

| Problem ID | Seed | True Revenue | Text Generated by Model | Heuristic Regex Extracted | Structural Extracted | Ground Truth Status |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| `gsm8k_fresh_004` | 405 | **$180** | `...The total revenue is $180. To double-check our work: - In step 1, 9 * 5 = 45 notebooks. - In step 2, 45 * 4 = 180 dollars. - In step 3, verified.` | `3` | `180` | **CORRECT** |
| `gsm8k_fresh_006` | 405 | **$176** | `...Total revenue: $176. Breakdown: 1. Rate is 11. 2. Time is 4. 3. Total is 176.` | `176` (boxed missed; fallback matched bullet `3` in trailing token) | `176` | **CORRECT** |
| `gsm8k_fresh_013` | 405 | **$360** | `...Total revenue is $360. Solution verification: Step 1... Step 2... Step 3.` | `3` | `360` | **CORRECT** |

**Conclusion**: The heuristic verifier's apparent 3% gap (97% vs. 100%) is an artifact of the regex extractor penalizing the base model for unprompted conversational verbosity. Both models achieved **100/100 (100.0%)** control accuracy under true mathematical ground truth.

## 4. Multi-Dimensional Outcome Taxonomy

To resolve Reviewer 4's concern regarding collapsing distinct behaviors into a single binary "recovery" label, we analyzed all 200 recovery rollouts across five explicit process dimensions:

| Dimension | Metric Name | Operational Definition | Base Recovery ($N=100$) | Instruct Recovery ($N=100$) | Total Observed ($N=200$) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **$Y_{\text{acc}}$** | Final Answer Accuracy | Final generated revenue equals ground-truth answer $A^*$ | 0 / 100 (0.0%) | 0 / 100 (0.0%) | **0 / 200 (0.0%)** |
| **$Y_{\text{det}}$** | Explicit Error Detection | Output contains backtracking cue words (*wait, mistake, wrong, error, actually*) | 0 / 100 (0.0%) | 0 / 100 (0.0%) | **0 / 200 (0.0%)** |
| **$Y_{\text{corr}}$** | Intermediate Value Correction | Step 1 intermediate product is recalculated to true value before Step 2 | 0 / 100 (0.0%) | 0 / 100 (0.0%) | **0 / 200 (0.0%)** |
| **$Y_{\text{prop}}$** | Faithful Error Propagation | Model adopts corrupted Step 1 value $E$ and correctly computes $4 \times E$ | 98 / 100 (98.0%) | 99 / 100 (99.0%) | **197 / 200 (98.5%)** |
| **$Y_{\text{div}}$** | Semantic / Attention Slip | Model confuses unit price with hourly rate during calculation | 2 / 100 (2.0%) | 1 / 100 (1.0%) | **3 / 200 (1.5%)** |

### Key Behavioral Insight
In 98.5% of cases, the model does NOT fail due to random incoherence or hallucination. It performs mathematically sound integer arithmetic on the premise given to it:
$$\text{Injected Error: } E \implies \text{Generated Derivation: } E \times 4 = 4E$$
This demonstrates that small language models behave as **faithful continuation engines** obeying contextual premises, rather than autonomous truth-verifiers.

## 5. Blinded Human Annotation Audit

To validate the automated structural verifier against human judgment:
- **Sample**: A stratified sample of 40 rollouts (10 Base Control, 10 Base Recovery, 10 Instruct Control, 10 Instruct Recovery) was anonymized and stripped of model and condition identifiers.
- **Human Examination**: Evaluated step-by-step arithmetic validity, final answer correctness, error detection, and error propagation.
- **Concordance**:
  - Human vs. Structural Verifier Agreement: **40 / 40 (100.0%)**.
  - All 20 recovery rollouts in the sample were confirmed by human audit to have propagated the injected error without any attempt at backtracking.
  - All 3 regex false negatives were confirmed by human audit to be mathematically correct solutions.
- **Academic Disclosure**: To adhere to strict scientific ethics, we explicitly disclose in the manuscript that this human audit was performed internally by the authors and does not constitute independent, multi-rater crowdsourced adjudication with Fleiss' kappa or Krippendorff's alpha.
