## 1. Description of the Baseline Defect

In the Phase XVII manuscript, Section 8 (Limitations) made the following assertion:
> *"Blinded human audit of 40 rollouts showed 100% concordance with structural parsing, but multi-rater crowdsourced adjudication remains pending future work."*

### Forensic Investigation:
1. **Search for Primary Evidence**:
   - The repository was searched for human annotator agreements, inter-rater reliability scores (Cohen's $\kappa$, Fleiss' $\kappa$), annotator timestamps, and external rater consent forms.
   - Found: `research_mission_01/phase_11_scientific_falsification/10_BLINDED_HUMAN_ANNOTATION_PACKET.csv` and `10_HUMAN_ANNOTATION_RUBRIC.md`.
2. **Analysis of the Artifacts**:
   - `10_BLINDED_HUMAN_ANNOTATION_PACKET.csv` is a stratified sample of 40 rollouts (10 Base Control, 10 Base Recovery, 10 Instruct Control, 10 Instruct Recovery) generated by random seed 42.
   - The columns include: `packet_id`, `model`, `condition`, `prompt_text`, `generated_text`, `target_gold_answer`.
   - The packet was prepared by the research team to serve as an evaluation protocol.
   - An internal inspection of these 40 items was conducted by the author to verify AST parser logic.
   - **Crucial Finding**: No independent, third-party, crowdsourced multi-rater study had been conducted. Claiming a "blinded human audit" implies an external human evaluation study that was never formally executed.

---

## 2. Research Integrity Violation Assessment

Under standard scientific publishing ethics (e.g., IEEE, ACM, NeurIPS):
- Describing an internal author sanity check or an unexecuted protocol as a completed "blinded human audit" is a **material misrepresentation of empirical evidence**.
- Conflating the *preparation* of a blinded packet with the *completion* of an external human study violates research integrity standards.

---

## 3. Rectification Implemented in Phase XVIII

1. **Complete Removal of the Misleading Claim**:
   The claim of a "blinded human audit showing 100% concordance" has been excised from the manuscript.
2. **Replacement with Factual Disclosure**:
   The limitation in Section 8 now explicitly states:
   > *"Human Validation Scope: An author audit of a stratified 40-item sample was conducted to calibrate AST parser logic. A standardized 40-item blinded annotation packet (\path{10_BLINDED_HUMAN_ANNOTATION_PACKET.csv}) and 6-criteria rubric are archived for open reproducibility; formal crowdsourced multi-rater inter-rater reliability (IRR) has not been conducted and remains pending."*
3. **Artifact Archiving**:
   Both the 40-item blinded packet and the 6-criteria rubric remain archived in the repository as open-science resources for future independent human replication.
