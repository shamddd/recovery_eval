# 07: Final Manuscript and Publication Ethics Audit

## 1. Executive Summary

This audit evaluates the ethical, legal, computational, and disclosure compliance of the RecoveryEval manuscript and research artifacts for arXiv publication.

---

## 2. Ethical Compliance and Declarations

### 2.1 Human Subjects and IRB Status
- **Empirical Scope**: The study exclusively evaluates synthetic arithmetic derivations generated by publicly available neural language model checkpoints (`Qwen2.5-Math-1.5B`).
- **Declaration**: Zero human participants, human evaluations, or human behavioral data were involved. Consequently, Institutional Review Board (IRB) review was not required. The manuscript explicitly states this factual status without asserting fictional formal exemption determinations.

### 2.2 Model and Asset Licensing
- **Model Checkpoints**: `Qwen/Qwen2.5-Math-1.5B` and `Qwen/Qwen2.5-Math-1.5B-Instruct` are released under the Apache 2.0 License by Alibaba Cloud, permitting open academic evaluation and reproduction.
- **Dataset Redistribution**: The 400 recorded neural rollouts (`data/10_COMPLETE_QWEN_PROTOCOL2_ROLLOUTS.jsonl`) and the 20 benchmark problem definitions (`BALANCED_PROMPT_REGISTRY.json`) were generated prospectively by the research team and are released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0) license.
- **Source Code**: Released under the MIT License.

### 2.3 Compute and Carbon Accounting
- **Historical Generation**: 400 rollouts executed on a single consumer GPU (NVIDIA RTX 4090, 24 GB VRAM) under Protocol 2.0 with an aggregate runtime of ~5.5 minutes (< 0.05 kWh total energy consumption).
- **Reproduction Phase**: All Phase XXI and Phase XXII verification and clean-room evaluations executed strictly on local Apple Silicon M1 CPU hardware consuming 0 cloud GPU hours and **USD $0.00** additional cloud expenditure.

### 2.4 AI Assistance Disclosure
- In accordance with standard publication ethics policies (ACM, IEEE, NeurIPS), the use of AI agentic tools for script generation, LaTeX compilation assistance, and visual page formatting is explicitly disclosed in the manuscript and repository documentation. All scientific claims, mathematical formulations, and empirical findings were directed and verified by Principal Investigator Sham Satish Thakare.

### 2.5 Conflict of Interest & Funding
- The author declares no competing financial or non-financial interests that could have influenced the work.
- The research was independently conducted with zero external corporate or grant funding.

## 3. Certification Verdict

The manuscript and repository meet all international standards of research integrity, disclosure, and ethical compliance.
