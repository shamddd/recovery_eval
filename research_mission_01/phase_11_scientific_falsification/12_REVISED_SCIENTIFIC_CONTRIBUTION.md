## 2. The Integrated Scientific Contribution (The B-C-D-E Synthesis)

The optimal, publication-grade contribution synthesizes Options B, C, D, and E into a coherent narrative:

### 2.1 The Methodological Contribution (Framework Architecture)
We propose `recovery_eval`, a data-centric evaluation methodology that isolates intermediate reasoning behavior by prospectively matching perturbed (recovery) and unperturbed (control) reasoning states on pre-treatment structural covariates (trajectory depth, remaining length, and token complexity). Combined with append-only exposure ledgers and byte-level rollout provenance, it provides a rigorous blueprint for diagnostic evaluation of reasoning models.

### 2.2 The Cautionary Methodological Discovery (Benchmark Governance)
We demonstrate how standard language model evaluation practices can fabricate phantom cognitive phenomena. Under naive evaluation with unbalanced prefixes and a 64-token budget, an apparent negative interaction ($D = -0.21$) emerged, erroneously suggesting that instruction tuning degrades recovery. When token budgets are expanded to eliminate truncation censoring and prompt prefix lengths are state-matched, this negative interaction dissolves.

### 2.3 The Empirical Discovery (Continuation Inertia)
Across 400 genuine neural rollouts generated by released Qwen2.5-Math-1.5B checkpoints, we observe that neither Base nor Instruct models spontaneously backtrack or self-correct from an injected intermediate arithmetic error ($p_{BR} = p_{IR} = 0.0000$). Instead, models exhibit **continuation inertia**: in 98.5% of cases, they accept the corrupted intermediate premise as authoritative and faithfully propagate it through downstream calculations.

### 2.4 The Evaluator Artifact Discovery (Parser Sensitivity)
We identify an evaluation failure mode where heuristic regex extractors that parse the "last numerical token" penalize unconstrained base models (which generate trailing explanatory notes concluding in step indices), inducing an artificial 3% deficit ($D = -0.0300, p = 0.0828$). Under deterministic structural arithmetic equivalence, both models achieve identical 100% control accuracy and 0% recovery ($D = 0.0000$).
