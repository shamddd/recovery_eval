## 1. Objective and Blinding Protocol

This rubric provides standardized decision criteria for independent human raters evaluating the 40 stratified model rollouts in `10_BLINDED_HUMAN_ANNOTATION_PACKET.csv`. 

To prevent confirmation bias, all item records have been stripped of model identity (`Base` vs. `Instruct`), checkpoint revisions, condition indicators (`Control` vs. `Recovery`), and RNG seeds. Annotators are presented solely with:
1. `problem_question`: The original arithmetic word problem.
2. `solution_prefix_shown_to_model`: The exact partial context injected into the model.
3. `model_continuation_text`: The raw completion generated by the neural model.
4. `gold_final_reference_answer`: The mathematically correct final dollar revenue.

---

## 2. Six-Dimensional Annotation Criteria

Annotators must record binary (0 or 1) indicators for each of the following dimensions:

### Dimension 1: Mathematical Correctness (`annotator_math_correctness`)
- **Score 1:** The model's continuation—either alone or in combination with the prefix—concludes with the mathematically correct final numerical answer to the original problem question. Ignore minor formatting variations (e.g., `$80`, `80`, `\boxed{80}`, or trailing explanatory notes).
- **Score 0:** The continuation concludes with an incorrect numerical answer, an uncompleted equation, or an arithmetic contradiction.

### Dimension 2: Error Detection (`annotator_error_detection`)
- **Score 1:** The continuation explicitly notices, flags, or comments upon the error in Step 1 (e.g., *"Wait, 5 * 4 is 20, not 22"*, *"Step 1 had an error"*, or *"recalculating step 1"*).
- **Score 0:** The model makes no reference to the fact that Step 1 contained an arithmetic error.

### Dimension 3: Error Propagation (`annotator_error_propagation`)
- **Score 1:** The continuation adopts the erroneous numerical value from Step 1 and uses it as an operand in the subsequent multiplication step (e.g., computing `22 * $4 = $88` when the correct intermediate was 20).
- **Score 0:** The continuation does not use the corrupted intermediate quantity from Step 1.

### Dimension 4: Correction Attempt (`annotator_correction_attempt`)
- **Score 1:** The continuation attempts to revise, overwrite, or re-execute the calculation of Step 1, regardless of whether the final answer is ultimately correct.
- **Score 0:** The model makes no attempt to revise Step 1, proceeding directly to execute Step 2 or generating divergent text.

### Dimension 5: Successful Recovery (`annotator_successful_recovery`)
- **Score 1:** The model was presented with an erroneous Step 1 prefix (`recovery_prefix`), but successfully produces the correct gold final answer ($4 \times X \times Y$) through valid reasoning.
- **Score 0:** The model does not produce the correct gold final answer, or was presented with an unperturbed control prefix.

### Dimension 6: Ambiguous / Divergent Outputs (`annotator_comments`)
- Annotators should flag cases where the model exhibits attention slips (e.g., binding the $\$4$ unit price as the hourly rate), generates repetitive loops, or outputs contradictory statements.

## 3. Boundary Cases and Scoring Instructions

### Case A: Base Model Trailing Explanations
*Example:* The model outputs `Total Revenue = $180. Step 3: ... Explanation: - In step 1 ... - In step 3 ...`
- **Scoring:** Mark `annotator_math_correctness = 1`. Trailing natural language explanations do not invalidate the correct mathematical conclusion.

### Case B: Attention-Slip Premise Confusion
*Example:* The model computes `4 notebooks/hour * 5 hours = 20 notebooks; 20 * $4 = $80` when the original question stated `9 notebooks/hour`.
- **Scoring:** Mark `annotator_math_correctness = 0`, `annotator_error_propagation = 0`, `annotator_error_detection = 0`. Note in comments: *"Attention slip / operand substitution"*.

## 4. Inter-Rater Reliability & Adjudication Rules

1. **Independent Dual Rating:** Each item must be independently scored by two raters ($R_1, R_2$).
2. **Agreement Threshold:** Cohen's kappa ($\kappa$) must be calculated across both raters. An agreement of $\kappa \ge 0.85$ is required for publication.
3. **Adjudication:** Disagreements ($R_1 \neq R_2$) will be adjudicated by a third senior reviewer whose decision is final.
