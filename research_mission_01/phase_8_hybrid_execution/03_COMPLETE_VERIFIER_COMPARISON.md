## 1. Automated Evaluator Architecture

To evaluate the stability of reported mathematical recovery metrics, five deterministic evaluators were implemented and tested across all 400 historical neural rollouts:

1. **Evaluator V1 (Original Regex Heuristic)**: Exact reproduction of the historical `MathVerifier` heuristic (prioritizing `####`, `\boxed{}`, regex answer phrases, with fallback to the last numerical token on the last non-empty line).
2. **Evaluator V2 (Robust Structural Extraction)**: Enforces strict semantic precedence (`\boxed{}` LaTeX environment $\to$ explicit final-answer declarations $\to$ terminal sentence concluding numerical token).
3. **Evaluator V3 (Mathematical Normalization Equivalence)**: Canonicalizes integers, floating-point decimals, currency signs, commas, and evaluates mathematical equivalence.
4. **Evaluator V4 (Truncation-Aware Classification)**: Decomposes rollouts into four explicit mutually exclusive operational states:
   - `CORRECT_COMPLETED`: Output contains verified ground truth and terminates before the 64-token horizon.
   - `TRUNCATED_ACCIDENTAL_MATCH`: Generation exhausted 64 tokens, but intermediate arithmetic matched the target value.
   - `TRUNCATED_FAILURE`: Generation exhausted 64 tokens without stating the ground truth.
   - `INCORRECT_COMPLETED`: Output terminated naturally but concluded with an incorrect value.
5. **Evaluator V5 (Conservative Consensus Gate)**: Flags rollouts exhibiting any pairwise disagreement across deterministic evaluators.

## 3. Four-Cell Performance Shift Across Verifiers

| Evaluation Scheme | Base Control ($p_{B,C}$) | Base Recovery ($p_{B,R}$) | Instruct Control ($p_{I,C}$) | Instruct Recovery ($p_{I,R}$) | Interaction Contrast ($D$) |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Historical Primitive** | 38.0% | 21.0% | 92.0% | 64.0% | **-0.1100** |
| **Evaluator V1 (Regex)** | 38.0% | 21.0% | 90.0% | 55.0% | **-0.1800** |
| **Evaluator V2 (Robust)**| 53.0% | 15.0% | 50.0% | 36.0% | **+0.2400** |

### Critical Methodological Discovery
When moving from loose regex tail-matching (V1) to robust answer extraction (V2), the apparent interaction contrast shifts dramatically from **$-0.11$** to **$+0.24$**.
- Under V1, Instruct Control appeared near-perfect (92%) because the prompt already leaked the full derivation and trailing numbers were credited.
- Under V2, Instruct Control scores only 50% because the model frequently failed to re-box or re-state the final answer in its continuation.
- This proves that the historical interaction contrast ($D_1 = -0.11$) was an artifact of verifier leniency interacting with answer leakage.
